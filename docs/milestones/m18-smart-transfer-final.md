Direct PostgreSQL snapshots show unchanged balances and transfer counts. Its [registered run](../../artifacts/m18-smart-actions-run-history/22131535d9c24770863f85e4d62e282b/) is retained.

Its [failed trace](../../artifacts/m18-smart-actions-model-smoke.failed-20261008-01.json) remains available. Contract revision 1.23.1 removed the unconditional list minimum; the server still requires participants for `CREATE`. The [failed lifecycle run](../../artifacts/m18-smart-actions-run-history/7a10f74dcf294346a9e53178cdfe1dd1/) and passing rerun are both retained. Passing reruns do not replace failures in prospective success metrics.

They are curated from separate runs, not a single prospective 100-case suite. The six new focused episodes above are separately registered and are not retroactively counted as a 100-case benchmark.
- Java Testcontainers tests cover schedule cancellation/confirmation serialization, transfer-prepare replay, AA participant visibility, creation/cancellation, no debit, replay, and concurrent confirmation.
